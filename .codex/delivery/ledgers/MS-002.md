## Iteration records

No product iteration has run. After each actual attempt, the runner links the
immutable report/checks/receipt or a precise decision packet and uses the exclusive
updater to record the result. Do not pre-fill passing outcomes. Each stage uses
its contract's report/receipt paths; no second execution register is created.

S01–S04 have technical acceptance; S05 additionally requires the owner's actual
acceptance of installed behavior and pilot-derived UI. A completed stage does not
automatically start its successor. Final acceptance has no next stage; C04 is a
separately selected future milestone. All user-facing executor reports are Russian.
