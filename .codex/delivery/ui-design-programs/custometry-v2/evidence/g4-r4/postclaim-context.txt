## Decision policy

- `agent_decidable`: Resolve reversible technical and presentation choices inside the accepted product and visual envelope; generate paths, hashes, receipts, ledgers, and routine writes without asking the user.
- `owner_required`: Ask only for missing product meaning or scope, new mobile scope, materially different visual direction, irreversible or costly choices, and acceptance or correction of a finished visual result.
- `external_authority`: Use an already-authorized repository route; hard-block only when a required permission or external state is genuinely unavailable.
- `needs_input`: Ledger-only non-terminal state for a material product question or finished-result review; resume the same row from typed owner-response evidence.
- `review_ready`: Machine evidence is complete and the finished result may be shown to the user; the stage remains non-terminal until the user accepts or requests a bounded revision.
- `blocked`: Terminal only for authority, safety, evidence, permission, inseparable foreign change, or unavailable external state.
